Agenda Math

An agenda adds up. The gaps between the items count too.

Boundary: arithmetic only Agenda Math adds the times, durations and gaps you type, in whole minutes on a 24-hour clock. It does not say how long an item should take, whether a plan is realistic, or what anyone has agreed to. You decide that.

The end truth

09:30 plus 90 minutes: 11:00. Past midnight, the answer carries the day, like 01:00 (+1 day).

The run truth

Start 09:00, gap 5 minutes: 09:00 to 09:30; 09:35 to 10:05; 10:10 to 10:40. Each item starts after the last one and its gap.

The fit truth

Window 09:00 to 12:00 is 180 minutes. Items and gaps need 155. Spare: 25. When the plan runs over, it says how many minutes short.

Run your numbers
Agenda Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
